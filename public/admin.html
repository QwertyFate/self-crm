Provision a new workspace

Creates the workspace, its owner and a ready-to-use inbound webhook in one go. Only the workspace name and the owner's email are required — everything else is optional. Custom fields are seeded into this new workspace only; afterwards its own members manage them in Settings.

Contact custom fields

Stored on every contact in this workspace, and mapped into the inbound webhook automatically.

Deal custom fields

Available on the deal board and the deal modal. The inbound webhook does not fill these — an auto-created deal gets a title, pipeline and stage only.

Workspaces & owners

Every workspace on the platform, newest first. "Provisioned" means it was created here; workspaces that predate this panel show "—" because nothing was backfilled.
